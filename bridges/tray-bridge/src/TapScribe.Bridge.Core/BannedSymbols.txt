T:System.Runtime.InteropServices.COMException; ExternalException is the capture seam's declared native failure type (IAudioDeviceEnumerator, IAudioCapture). COMException is one Windows backend's subclass of it, so a filter naming it stops catching the moment the backend is Core Audio: the throw escapes a teardown documented as throw-free, or a dead device sinks the whole meeting instead of being skipped. Catch ExternalException.
