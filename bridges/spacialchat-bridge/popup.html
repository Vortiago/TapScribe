Meeting

Starts a bracketed recording in its own detached session on the recorder and routes every speaker you tap into it — no dashboard needed. The whole meeting stays in one session, even across SpatialChat room changes, until you end it. End meeting flushes any trailing audio, closes the taps, and kicks off the recorder's strip → transcribe → summarize pipeline for that session.
